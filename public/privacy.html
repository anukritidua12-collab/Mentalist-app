Privacy policy

MentaList · Effective 20 October 2026

In short

  • Your tasks, lists, notes and attachments are stored on your device. They are not sent to us.
  • MentaList has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • Information only leaves your device when you choose to share it, using an app or service you pick.

Who we are

MentaList is made by Anukriti Dua (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the MentaList app for iPhone and the MentaList web app (together, “MentaList”) handle information. It applies to the current version of MentaList.

Information stored on your device

To work as a to-do list, MentaList stores what you enter in it: tasks and subtasks, lists, notes, dates, reminder times, priorities, photos and files you attach, and your settings (such as your theme and reminder sound).

This information is kept in MentaList’s private storage on your device (on iPhone), or in your browser’s storage on your computer (in the web app). It is not uploaded to us or to any server operated by us, and we cannot see, access or recover it.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through MentaList. In particular, MentaList does not:

  • ask you to create an account or sign in;
  • use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools;
  • collect your location, contacts, device identifiers or browsing activity;
  • track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Permissions MentaList may ask for

  • Notifications. Used to show the reminders you set. Reminders are scheduled on your device by its operating system; no reminder information is sent to us.
  • Photos, camera, microphone and files. Used only when you choose to attach something to a task. The item you pick, or the photo or video you take (the microphone is used only to record a video’s sound), is copied into MentaList’s storage on your device. MentaList cannot see your other photos or files.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device’s Settings app or your browser’s settings. MentaList keeps working without them; only the related feature stops.

When you share something

If you choose to share a task (for example with Share details), MentaList prepares the text and hands it to the app or service you select, such as Messages, Mail or WhatsApp. That app or service then handles the information under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive a copy.

Links to other apps and websites

Some tasks may contain links, or show a shortcut to another app (such as an email or messaging app). If you open one, you leave MentaList, and the other app or website handles your information under its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for the content or practices of other apps and websites.

The web app

When you open MentaList in a browser, the page is delivered by our hosting provider, which may process technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the page securely, as any website host does. MentaList itself does not set cookies. Your tasks are stored only in your browser on that device.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, your device may include MentaList’s data in those backups. Backups are managed by Apple and by your own settings, not by us.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual tasks, clear your History, or delete MentaList from your device, which removes its data from that device. Copies in device backups remain until those backups are replaced or deleted. Because we never receive your information, we cannot delete, export or restore it for you.

Security

Your information is protected by your device’s own security. We recommend using a passcode, Face ID or Touch ID on your device and keeping its software up to date. No method of storage is completely secure, and you are responsible for the security of your device.

Children

MentaList is not directed to children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age where you live. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and MentaList does not collect personal information from anyone.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal information about you, such as the right to access, correct or delete it, or to object to its use (for example under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation or the California Consumer Privacy Act). Because MentaList does not collect personal information, we do not hold any information about you to act on, and we do not sell or share personal information. You can exercise full control over your information directly on your device. If you have a question about your rights, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how MentaList handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date. If a future version collects personal information (for example to let you share tasks with other people), we will update this policy before that version is released, tell you in the app, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
