MentaList support

Help with MentaList, the calm to-do list for iPhone and the web.

Contact us

Questions, problems or ideas: write to mentalistishere@gmail.com. Every message is read, and we aim to reply within a few days.

It helps to mention your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn’t working.

Common questions

Where is the full guide?
Inside the app: Settings → Guide explains every feature, from quick add to repeating tasks and reminders.
My reminders don’t arrive.
Check that notifications are allowed in the iPhone Settings app → MentaList → Notifications, then try Settings → Reminders → Send test notification in MentaList. Focus modes can also hold notifications back.
How do I get the morning summary or evening wrap-up?
Turn them on in Settings → Reminders and choose a time. They’re off until you switch them on.
How do repeating tasks work?
In a task’s details, choose how often it repeats under Schedule. When you tick it off, the next one appears on its next date with the same notes, subtasks and attachments.
I deleted something by mistake.
Open History from the sidebar and choose Restore. Items stay there until you delete them forever or clear History.
Where is my data stored? Can I move it to a new phone?
Everything stays on your device; nothing is sent to us. Your tasks move to a new iPhone with an iCloud or computer backup of your phone.
Is my information private?
Yes. MentaList has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. See the privacy policy.
