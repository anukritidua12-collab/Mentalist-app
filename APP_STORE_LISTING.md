## Description (4000)

```
MentaList is a calm, fast to-do list that holds everything on your mind so you can get on with your day.

Add a task in a second, tick it off with a satisfying line, and watch your day settle into order. When something needs more, there's room for dates, reminders, subtasks, notes and files, without making everything else feel heavy.

YOUR DAY, AT A GLANCE
• Today brings together what matters now, with a clear view of how much is done
• Quick add: type a task and press return, and it's on the list
• Plan ahead: put tasks on future days, and they arrive when the day comes
• Suggestions for small things worth doing, refreshed through the day

LISTS FOR EVERYTHING
• Work, Personal and as many of your own lists as you like
• Quick lists work like a checklist in Notes: perfect for groceries, packing or errands
• One task can live in several lists and stays in step everywhere
• Priorities, subtasks, notes, photos and files

REMINDERS THAT REACH YOU
• Reminders arrive like any other notification, even when MentaList is closed
• Repeating tasks: every day, weekday, week, two weeks, month or year
• A morning summary of the day ahead, and an evening wrap-up of how it went (both optional)

A LITTLE CELEBRATION
• Every task you tick off gets a pen-stroke line through it
• Finished tasks gather neatly at the bottom
• Finish everything and MentaList marks the moment with confetti

MAKE IT YOURS
• Nine themes, from Clarity and Serenity to Midnight and true-black Ink
• Light, dark or follow your iPhone
• Text follows your iPhone's text size

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Everything stays on your iPhone; nothing is sent to us
• Share a task only when you choose, with any app you like

A Guide inside the app explains every feature. Questions or ideas? Write to mentalistishere@gmail.com.
```
